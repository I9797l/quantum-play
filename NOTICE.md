## What this repository does

It keeps an unmodified, byte-identical copy of the deployed build (hashes in `SHA256SUMS.txt`) as a
personal backup, and links to the live app: <https://quantum-play.replit.app/>. It adds no license and
grants no rights. The screenshots in `docs/screenshots/` are captures of that unmodified build.
